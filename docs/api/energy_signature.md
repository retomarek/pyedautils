# energy_signature

Proposed Energy Signature (PES) after Eriksson et al. (2020) — iterative
determination of balance temperature, heat loss coefficient, domestic hot
water circulation (DHWC) and domestic hot water (DHW) demand from hourly
time-series data.

See {doc}`../examples/energy_signature` for usage examples. The plotting
counterparts live in {doc}`plots`.
