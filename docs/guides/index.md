# Guides

Background for reading the comfort plots, with examples computed by
pyedautils itself.

- {doc}`mollier_hx` — The h,x diagram in five minutes: axes and curves, how
  heating, humidifying and ventilating move the point, dew point and mould,
  ventilating in winter, heating less, airing a cellar in summer.
- {doc}`comfort_compass` — Reading the comfort compass, comparing rooms, and
  what the arms mean for people and the environment.
